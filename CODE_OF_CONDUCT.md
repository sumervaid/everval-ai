# Community standards

These standards apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related discussions.

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas, code, and evidence without personal attacks.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication needs. Explain assumptions and keep feedback actionable.
- Discuss disagreements in good faith. State what you observed and what would change your conclusion.
- Respect privacy and permission to share information. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, or confidential work.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or repeatedly target other participants.

## Addressing a concern

Use your existing private project channel to contact the repository owner about a conduct concern. If you do not have a direct contact, ask the person who provided repository access to connect you. Share only the context needed to understand the concern; avoid reproducing sensitive material in an issue.

Repository maintainers may ask for edits, remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, or restrict participation to protect contributors and keep project work constructive. If the concern involves that contact, use another established project contact where available.

Technical disagreements belong in a focused issue or review. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).
